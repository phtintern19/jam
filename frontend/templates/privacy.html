Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2024

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

When you register and use BidZone, we collect:

  • Full name and contact information (email, phone)
  • Age and location data
  • Sports skills and performance ratings
  • Profile photos and biographical information
  • Communication preferences

Usage Information

We automatically collect:

  • Device information and browser type
  • IP address and location data
  • Pages visited and time spent on platform
  • Auction participation and bidding history
  • Communication logs and support interactions

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Facilitate player registration and profile creation
  • Match players with appropriate auction events
  • Enable team owners to evaluate and bid on players
  • Process payments and manage financial transactions
  • Send important notifications about events and opportunities
  • Improve platform functionality and user experience
  • Ensure platform security and prevent fraud
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

3. Information Sharing

With Team Owners

During auctions, we share relevant player information:

  • Profile details and skills assessments
  • Performance ratings and achievements
  • Contact information for successful bids
  • Biographical information provided during registration

With Service Providers

We may share information with:

  • Payment processors for transaction handling
  • Cloud hosting providers for data storage
  • Analytics services to improve platform performance
  • Communication services for notifications

Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when required by law or to:

  • Protect user safety and platform security
  • Respond to legal requests and court orders
  • Investigate fraud or illegal activities
  • Enforce our terms of service

4. Data Security

We implement comprehensive security measures:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmission
  • Secure cloud storage with access controls
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability testing
  • Employee access restrictions on a need-to-know basis
  • Incident response procedures for data breaches

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Access and Control

You have the right to:

  • Access and review your personal information
  • Update or correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Opt-out of marketing communications

Marketing Communications

You can control marketing emails through:

  • Unsubscribe links in email communications
  • Account settings and preferences
  • Contacting our support team

6. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your login status and preferences
  • Analyze website traffic and usage patterns
  • Improve platform functionality and user experience
  • Provide personalized content and recommendations
  • Ensure platform security and prevent fraud

You can control cookies through your browser settings, though this may affect platform functionality.

7. Data Retention

We retain your information for:

  • Active accounts: As long as your account remains active
  • Legal requirements: As required by applicable laws
  • Business purposes: For legitimate business needs
  • Dispute resolution: Until disputes are resolved

When you delete your account, we will remove your personal information within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place:

  • Adequacy decisions by relevant authorities
  • Standard contractual clauses
  • Binding corporate rules
  • Certification schemes

9. Third-Party Services

Our platform may integrate with third-party services:

  • Payment processors (Stripe, PayPal, etc.)
  • Social media platforms for sharing
  • Analytics services (Google Analytics)
  • Communication tools (email services)

These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

10. Children's Privacy

BidZone is not intended for children under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If we become aware that we have collected such information, we will take steps to delete it immediately.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes through:

  • Email notifications
  • Website announcements
  • Updated "Last modified" date

Your continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or concerns, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@bidzone.com
  • Phone: +91-9999999999
  • Address: BidZone Privacy Officer, New Delhi, India

We will respond to privacy inquiries within 30 days.
